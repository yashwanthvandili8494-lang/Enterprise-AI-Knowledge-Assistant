# ACME CORP - INFORMATION SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE PROTOCOL (SEC-SOP-009)

## 1. Purpose & Incident Definition
This protocol defines standard operating procedures for identifying, triaging, containing, eradicating, and reporting information security incidents affecting Acme Corp infrastructure, customer data, or employee workstations.

An incident is defined as any confirmed or suspected adverse event that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Acme systems or data.

## 2. Severity Classification Matrix
- SEV-1 (Critical Incident):
  * Scope: Confirmed breach of customer personally identifiable information (PII), active ransomware outbreak, unauthorized administrative access to production AWS/GCP accounts, or uncontained remote code execution.
  * Response SLA: Initial responder engagement within 15 minutes, 24/7.
  * Command: Head of Information Security and CISO take direct command.

- SEV-2 (Major Incident):
  * Scope: Isolated malware infection on internal network, denial of service (DoS) impacting non-critical services, or compromised internal employee credentials without administrative privilege.
  * Response SLA: Responder engagement within 45 minutes during business hours or 2 hours off-hours.

- SEV-3 (Minor Incident):
  * Scope: Phishing email reported with no link clicks, policy violation, or failed brute-force attacks against firewalls.
  * Response SLA: Responder engagement within 4 business hours.

## 3. Reporting Channels & Immediate Action
1. All staff who identify an anomaly or suspect an unauthorized access event must report it IMMEDIATELY via:
   - Security Incident Bot in Slack: `/report-incident`
   - Dedicated Emergency Hotline: Ext. 4433 (or +1-800-555-ACME)
   - Email: `security-team@acme.com`
2. DO NOT power off affected machines. Disconnect Ethernet cables or disable Wi-Fi to isolate the host while preserving volatile RAM state for forensic extraction.

## 4. Containment and Eradication Procedures
- Network Isolation: SecOps isolates affected VPC subnets using AWS security group revokes.
- Credential Revocation: All active sessions for compromised identities are revoked in Okta and Azure AD within 10 minutes.
- Evidence Preservation: Memory dumps (`dd` or Volatility capture) and disk volume snapshots are preserved with SHA-256 hashes for chain-of-custody.

## 5. Regulatory Notification Timelines
- GDPR & European Authorities: Under Article 33 of GDPR, notifications to supervising data protection authorities must be made within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach.
- Customer Notification: Impacted customers must be notified in writing within 5 calendar days following containment verification, coordinated by Legal and Communications departments.
